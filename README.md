# Sunbreak Coastline

An open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser from plain static files. Explore a sunlit coastal city on foot or behind the wheel, take what you need, outrun the law, and work your way through five story missions.

## How to play

Serve it locally, then open the game in your browser:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. You can also open `game/index.html` directly. A desktop browser with a keyboard is recommended, since the game is built around keyboard controls.

Press Enter on the title screen to start a new game.

## Controls

- WASD: move on foot, drive when in a car
- E: interact, enter or exit a car
- Enter: start a new game from the title screen

## Features

- A coastal city to roam, with streets, traffic, and pedestrians
- Cars you can steal and drive
- A wanted level with police chases that escalate as you cause trouble
- Five story missions that take you across the city
- Combat on foot and from vehicles
- A HUD with health, wanted level, and a minimap
